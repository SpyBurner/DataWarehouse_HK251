# Feature Engineering Documentation

This document describes the features generated by the `features.py` script. The goal is to migrate these calculations from Pandas (reading CSVs) to a Pentaho pipeline reading from the Postgres Data Warehouse (`dw` schema) and loading into the `dm.dm_steam_player_features_v1` table.

## Group A: Speed Features
Focuses on the speed and rhythm of achievement unlocks.

### 1. `median_unlock_interval_sec`, `std_unlock_interval_sec`, `cv_unlock_interval`
- **What it is:** Statistics of the time differences (in seconds) between consecutive achievement unlocks for a player.
- **How it's calculated:** Sort `fact_achievement_unlock` by `playerid` and `date_acquired`. Calculate the time difference tracking row-to-row per player. Then aggregate to find median, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:** 
  1. `Table Input` (SQL: `LEAD()` or `LAG()` window function over `date_acquired` partitioned by `playerid`).
  2. `Group By` / `Memory Group By` (calculate Median and Standard Deviation).
  3. `Calculator` (compute CV = std / mean).

### 2. `max_achievements_per_minute`, `max_achievements_per_day`
- **What it is:** The maximum number of achievements a player has unlocked in a single minute and a single day.
- **How it's calculated:** Truncate `date_acquired` to minute/day. Count achievements per trunc-period per player, then take the max.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (using Postgres `date_trunc('minute', date_acquired)`).
  2. `Group By` (count rows per player, per minute/day).
  3. `Group By` (max count per player).

## Group B: Temporal Features
Focuses on time-of-day and activity patterns, adjusted for the player's time zone.

### 1. `night_activity_ratio`
- **What it is:** Percentage of achievements unlocked during local night hours (00:00 - 05:59).
- **How it's calculated:** Map player country to UTC offset, add offset to UTC hour, apply modulo 24. Count unlocks where local hour < 6, divide by total unlocks.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (join `fact_achievement_unlock` and `dim_player`, mapping country to offset via `Value Mapper` or standard SQL `CASE`).
  2. `Calculator` (mod 24 math for local hour).
  3. `Filter Rows` (isolate night hours).
  4. `Group By` (count total and night unlocks, compute ratio).

### 2. `hour_entropy`
- **What it is:** Shannon entropy of the 24-hour unlock distribution (how evenly playtime is distributed across the day).
- **How it's calculated:** Count unlocks for each local hour (0-23) per player. Apply `scipy.stats.entropy`.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Difficult to do purely in native Pentaho steps without custom scripting.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:** 
  1. `Table Input` / `Group By` (Pivot to 24 columns for hour counts).
  2. `Modified Java Script Value` or `Python Evaluator` (to calculate the Shannon entropy math equation).

### 3. `activity_density`
- **What it is:** The ratio of days active out of the player's total calendar span of activity.
- **How it's calculated:** Count unique days active / (max_date - min_date in days + 1).
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (using Postgres `COUNT(DISTINCT date_acquired)` and `MAX(date_acquired) - MIN(date_acquired)` over `fact_achievement_unlock`).

## Group C: Diversity Features
Focuses on the spread of achievements across the player's game library.

### 1. `total_achievements`, `games_with_achievements`, `library_size`
- **What it is:** Counts of total unlocks, unique games played with unlocks, and total games owned.
- **How it's calculated:** Simple aggregations on `fact_achievement` and `fact_library` (or `dm_steam_player_features_v1` base tables).
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:** 
  1. `Table Input` (Group by aggregations `COUNT(*)` and `COUNT(DISTINCT gameid)`).

### 2. `achievement_game_ratio`, `avg_achievements_per_game`
- **What it is:** Ratio of games with achievements vs total library size, and average unlocks per game.
- **How it's calculated:** `games_with_achievements / library_size` and `total_achievements / games_with_achievements`.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Calculator` (divide the standard metric fields).

### 3. `top1_game_concentration`, `top3_game_concentration`, `game_hhi`
- **What it is:** Concentration of unlocks in the top 1/top 3 games, and Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (sum of squared proportions).
- **How it's calculated:** Proportion of unlocks per game vs total unlocks. Sum top N or sum squares.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (Rank games by count per player using `ROW_NUMBER()` in SQL).
  2. `Filter Rows` (Keep top 1 or top 3).
  3. `Group By` (Sum proportions).

## Group D: Review Features
Focuses on review behavior signals.

### 1. `total_reviews`, `avg_review_length`, `min_review_length`
- **What it is:** Volume and character lengths of written reviews.
- **How it's calculated:** Count reviews, calculate length of review text.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (Read `fact_review`).
  2. `Group By` (Compute COUNT, Strings length average and min).

### 2. `review_unplayed_ratio`
- **What it is:** Fraction of reviews written for games where the playtime is 0.
- **How it's calculated:** Intersect reviewed games with library games that have 0 playtime.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes (Assuming playtime data is ingested into the DW).
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (Join `fact_review` and the source table for library playtime).
  2. `Filter Rows` (playtime = 0).
  3. `Group By` (Count unplayed reviews vs total reviews).

### 3. `review_duplication_rate`
- **What it is:** Rate of duplicated review text per player.
- **How it's calculated:** `1.0 - (UNIQUE(lowered strings) / TOTAL(reviews))`.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `String Operations` (Lower case, trim).
  2. `Group By` (Count distinct vs Count total).
  3. `Calculator` (Subtraction and division).

## Group E: Account Age Features
Focuses on the lifecycle span.

### 1. `days_before_first_achievement`, `account_age_days`
- **What it is:** Days between account creation and first unlock, and total account age.
- **How it's calculated:** MIN(date_acquired) - date_created, and NOW() - date_created.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (Postgres date math `MIN(date_acquired) - created`).

## Group F: Playtime Features
Focuses on assessing the plausibility of unlocked achievements based on logged playtime.
*(Note: Current DW Postgres schema for `fact_library` lacks `playtime_mins`. The pipeline must extend `fact_library` or stage to support these).*

### 1. `zero_playtime_achievements_ratio`
- **What it is:** The ratio of achievements unlocked on games with 0 registered playtime over all valid games.
- **How it's calculated:** Count achievements joined with library where `playtime = 0`, divided by achievements with `playtime >= 0`.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (Join `fact_achievement_unlock` with library for playtime).
  2. `Filter Rows` / `Group By` (Compute zero-playtime bounds).

### 2. `playtime_per_achievement`, `total_playtime_mins`
- **What it is:** Total playtime across played games, divided by valid achievements.
- **How it's calculated:** Sum of `playtime_mins` across DISTINCT games in library where playtime > 0.
- **Pentaho Possibility:** Yes.
- **Pentaho Minimal Steps:**
  1. `Table Input` (Deduplicate game playtimes per player).
  2. `Group By` (Sum playtime).
  3. `Calculator` (Divide total playtime by available accomplishments).
